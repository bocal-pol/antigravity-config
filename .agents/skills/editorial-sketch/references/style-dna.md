# Style DNA

## In one sentence

Pure white, minimalist, hand-drawn, airy, sober, strange, product-sketch spirit, handwritten touch,
clear structure but never didactic.

As if someone who has long worked in AI, product, or tool design were freehand-sketching an
explanatory drawing on a blank sheet of paper.

## Mandatory

- Horizontal 16:9 companion illustration
- Pure white background: no beige, no warm gray, no paper texture, no gradient, no shadow, no
  noise, no vintage effect
- Predominantly hand-drawn black line: thin, slightly shaky, neither mechanical nor vector-like,
  never too thick
- Lots of white space: the subject occupies about 40-60% of the image, at least 35% empty space,
  ideally one complete calm zone
- Few handwritten annotations: 5 to 8 spots maximum, 2 to 8 characters/words per spot
- One image tells only one central action, structure, state, or metaphor
- The structure must express itself naturally — never write the structure type's name on the image

## Colors

- Black: main subject, character, frame, structure, main text, main object
- Red: key annotation, problem, emotional point, important warning, result
- Orange: main flow, path, arrow, automation direction, movement from A to B
- Blue: complementary explanation, internal state, system state, second level of explanation,
  AI/assistant/automation hint

Blue isn't mandatory on every image. Stay sober on color — too little is better than too much.

## Absolutely forbidden

- Commercial illustration
- PPT infographic
- Formal flowchart
- Course slide
- Cute cartoon poster
- Children's illustration
- Complex architecture diagram
- Polished flat-design illustration
- High-tech interface
- Real app screenshot
- Complex background, gradient, shadow, texture
- Explaining every node in detail
- A title like "Workflow / Architecture diagram / Common pitfalls / Roadmap" in the top-left corner

## Aesthetic direction

Strange, creative, interesting, uncluttered, sober, a little off-kilter.

Never cute, never childish, never overloaded, never rigid.
