# Collectible figurine

## Effect
Turns the subject into a collectible figurine with visible packaging, collectible-product style.

Also covers the popular variants **actionfigure** (articulated figure with accessories),
**funko** (large-head chibi vinyl style) and **toybox** (open toy box) — specify the
variant in Variable 2.

## Imposed structure (non-negotiable)
- Stylized figurine of the subject on a display stand
- Blister/box packaging with a clear window and a generic placeholder logo
- Studio product lighting, detailed plastic/vinyl texture

## Positive Prompt (English skeleton)
`collectible figurine of {{subject}}, stylized toy sculpt on display stand, retail blister
packaging box with clear window and generic logo placeholder beside it, studio product
photography lighting, high detail plastic/vinyl texture`

## Negative Prompt
`unpackaged only, realistic human proportions, watermark, blurry`

## Variables to collect
1. Subject to turn into a figurine?
2. Variant (large-head chibi vinyl / articulated action figure / static vinyl)?

## Combinable with
- `knolling`, `lego`

## Example
Subject "the family cat" → chibi vinyl style figurine with a "Collector Series" box.
