# Before/after — Beforeafter

## Effect
Two-panel transformation, same framing, initial state vs. final state.

## Imposed structure (non-negotiable)
- Two panels with identical framing and angle
- Only the subject's state changes between the two
- Explicit "before"/"after" labels

## Positive Prompt (English skeleton)
`before and after transformation of {{subject}}, two panels with identical framing and angle,
only the subject's state changes, labeled "before" and "after", consistent lighting`

## Negative Prompt
`different camera angle between panels, inconsistent lighting, watermark, blurry`

## Variables to collect
1. Subject and transformation (renovation, diet, growth...)?
2. Initial and final state to describe?

## Combinable with
- `comparison`, `timeline`

## Example
Subject "abandoned garden" → before overgrown, after landscaped.
