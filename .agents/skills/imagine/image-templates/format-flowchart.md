# Flowchart — Flowchart

## Effect
Process flowchart with standardized shapes and directional arrows.

## Imposed structure (non-negotiable)
- Standard shapes: oval start/end, diamond decision, rectangle action
- Vertical or horizontal flow, numbered arrows
- White background, professional diagram style

## Positive Prompt (English skeleton)
`process flowchart of {{subject}}, standard flowchart shapes (oval start/end, diamond decisions,
rectangle actions), directional arrows, clean vertical flow, professional diagram style, white
background`

## Negative Prompt
`non-standard shapes, missing arrows, cluttered, watermark, blurry`

## Variables to collect
1. Process to represent?
2. Presence of decision points (yes/no)?

## Combinable with
- `diagram`, `timeline`

## Example
Subject "support ticket validation process" → steps with an "escalate?" decision diamond.
